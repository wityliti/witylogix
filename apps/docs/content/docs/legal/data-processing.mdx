# Data Processing Agreement

**Effective Date:** March 26, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 26, 2026

This page provides a summary of the Witylogix Data Processing Agreement ("DPA"). The DPA governs the processing of personal data by Witylogix Inc. ("Witylogix," "Processor," "we," or "us") on behalf of our Merchants ("Controller," "you," or "your") in connection with the Witylogix platform and related services (the "Service").

This summary is provided for informational purposes. The full DPA is incorporated into and subject to our [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms-of-service). To request a signed copy of the full DPA, contact [legal@witylogix.io](mailto:legal@witylogix.io).

---

## 1. Scope of Processing

### 1.1 Subject Matter

Witylogix processes personal data on your behalf to provide delivery logistics services, including order management, route optimization, delivery tracking, and e-commerce platform synchronization.

### 1.2 Categories of Data Subjects

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Merchant personnel** | Employees and authorized users of the Merchant's Witylogix account |
| **End customers** | Individuals who place orders through the Merchant's sales channels |
| **Delivery drivers** | Individuals who perform deliveries on behalf of the Merchant |

### 1.3 Types of Personal Data Processed

| Data Type | Examples |
|-----------|---------|
| **Identity data** | Names, email addresses, phone numbers |
| **Contact data** | Delivery addresses, billing addresses |
| **Order data** | Order IDs, items ordered, order values, special instructions |
| **Location data** | Driver GPS coordinates during active deliveries |
| **Device data** | IP addresses, browser information (for platform users) |
| **Payment references** | Transaction IDs, payment status (no full card numbers) |

### 1.4 Duration of Processing

Processing begins when you activate the Service and continues until your account is terminated. After termination, data is retained and deleted in accordance with the retention schedule in our [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy).

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

### 2.1 Merchant as Controller

As the Data Controller, you are responsible for:

- Determining the purposes and means of processing personal data
- Ensuring a valid legal basis exists for all processing (e.g., consent, contract, legitimate interest)
- Providing appropriate privacy notices to data subjects (your customers, drivers, and personnel)
- Responding to data subject rights requests (with our assistance as Processor)
- Conducting data protection impact assessments where required
- Ensuring your instructions to Witylogix comply with applicable data protection laws

### 2.2 Witylogix as Processor

As the Data Processor, Witylogix is responsible for:

- Processing personal data only on your documented instructions
- Ensuring that persons authorized to process personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Implementing appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Assisting you in responding to data subject rights requests
- Assisting you with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities
- Notifying you of personal data breaches without undue delay
- Deleting or returning personal data upon termination of the Service
- Making available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allowing for audits

## 3. Processing Instructions

### 3.1 Documented Instructions

We process personal data only in accordance with your documented instructions. Instructions are provided through:

- The configuration and use of the Service (e.g., setting up integrations, creating delivery routes)
- The Terms of Service and this DPA
- Written instructions provided via email to [dpo@witylogix.io](mailto:dpo@witylogix.io)

### 3.2 Additional Instructions

If you require processing beyond the scope described in this DPA, you must provide written instructions. We may charge additional fees for processing that falls outside the standard scope of the Service.

### 3.3 Conflicting Instructions

If we believe an instruction violates the GDPR or other applicable data protection law, we will inform you promptly and may suspend the relevant processing until you confirm or modify the instruction.

## 4. Security Measures

Witylogix implements and maintains the following technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, in accordance with Article 32 of the GDPR:

### 4.1 Encryption

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Encryption at rest** | AES-256 encryption for all data stored in PostgreSQL databases and backup storage |
| **Encryption in transit** | TLS 1.2 or higher for all data transmitted between clients, services, and third parties |
| **Key management** | Encryption keys managed through dedicated key management services with automatic rotation |

### 4.2 Access Controls

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Authentication** | Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all administrative and production system access |
| **Authorization** | Role-based access control (RBAC) enforcing the principle of least privilege |
| **Access reviews** | Quarterly reviews of access permissions with prompt revocation upon role change or termination |
| **Session management** | Automatic session expiration and re-authentication requirements |

### 4.3 Infrastructure Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Network security** | Firewalls, network segmentation, and intrusion detection/prevention systems |
| **Vulnerability management** | Automated vulnerability scanning, regular penetration testing, and timely patching |
| **DDoS protection** | Distributed denial-of-service mitigation at the infrastructure level |
| **Logging and monitoring** | Comprehensive audit logging of all access to personal data and administrative actions |

### 4.4 Organizational Measures

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Confidentiality** | All employees and contractors sign confidentiality agreements |
| **Training** | Mandatory data protection and security awareness training upon hire and annually |
| **Background checks** | Background checks for personnel with access to production systems |
| **Incident response** | Documented incident response plan with regular testing and updates |
| **Business continuity** | Disaster recovery plan with regular backup and restoration testing |

## 5. Sub-processor Management

### 5.1 Authorization

You provide general written authorization for Witylogix to engage sub-processors to assist in providing the Service. The current list of sub-processors is maintained on our [GDPR Compliance](/docs/legal/gdpr) page.

### 5.2 Sub-processor Obligations

Before engaging any sub-processor, Witylogix ensures that:

- A written agreement is in place imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA
- The sub-processor implements appropriate technical and organizational measures
- The sub-processor is subject to confidentiality obligations

### 5.3 Change Notification

We will provide at least 30 days' prior written notice before engaging a new sub-processor or replacing an existing one. You may object to a new sub-processor within 15 days of receiving notice. If you object and we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, either party may terminate the affected portion of the Service.

### 5.4 Liability

Witylogix remains liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors to the same extent as if we had performed the processing directly.

## 6. Audit Rights

### 6.1 Information and Compliance

Witylogix will make available to you all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and applicable data protection laws.

### 6.2 Audits and Inspections

You have the right to conduct audits, including inspections, of our processing activities covered by this DPA. Audits are subject to the following conditions:

- **Frequency:** One audit per 12-month period, unless a data breach or supervisory authority requirement necessitates an additional audit
- **Notice:** At least 30 days' written notice
- **Scope:** Limited to processing activities relevant to the DPA
- **Conduct:** Audits may be conducted by you or an independent third-party auditor bound by confidentiality obligations
- **Cooperation:** Witylogix will provide reasonable cooperation, access to relevant facilities, and access to personnel
- **Costs:** Each party bears its own costs, except that you are responsible for the costs of auditors you engage

### 6.3 Certifications and Reports

In lieu of an on-site audit, we may provide:

- SOC 2 Type II audit reports (or equivalent)
- Relevant certifications and compliance documentation
- Responses to standardized security questionnaires

## 7. Data Subject Rights Assistance

### 7.1 Obligations

Witylogix will assist you in fulfilling your obligations to respond to data subject rights requests, including requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection.

### 7.2 Process

When we receive a data subject request directly:

1. We will promptly redirect the data subject to you (the Controller), unless you have instructed us otherwise
2. We will notify you of the request within 3 business days
3. We will provide technical assistance to fulfill the request upon your instruction

### 7.3 Technical Capabilities

The Service provides the following capabilities to support data subject rights:

| Right | Capability |
|-------|-----------|
| **Access** | Data export functionality in structured, machine-readable formats (JSON, CSV) |
| **Rectification** | Data editing through the dashboard and API |
| **Erasure** | Data deletion through the dashboard, API, or by request to support |
| **Restriction** | Ability to flag and restrict processing of specific records |
| **Portability** | Bulk data export in standard formats |

## 8. Data Deletion on Termination

### 8.1 Data Return

Upon termination of the Service, you may export your Merchant Data through the dashboard or API for a period of 30 days following the effective date of termination.

### 8.2 Deletion

After the 30-day export period:

1. We will delete all Merchant Data from our primary production systems within 30 days
2. Merchant Data in backups will be deleted within 90 days, or overwritten as backups are rotated
3. We will provide written confirmation of deletion upon request

### 8.3 Exceptions

We may retain personal data beyond the deletion timeline only where required by applicable law (e.g., tax records, legal proceedings). In such cases, we will:

- Inform you of the specific legal requirement
- Limit retention to the minimum data and duration required by law
- Continue to protect the data in accordance with this DPA

### 8.4 Anonymized Data

We may retain aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified data that cannot be used to identify any individual. Such data is no longer considered personal data under the GDPR.
