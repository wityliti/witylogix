# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 26, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 26, 2026

Witylogix Inc. ("Witylogix," "we," "us," or "our") operates a business-to-business (B2B) software-as-a-service platform that provides delivery logistics solutions to merchants ("Merchants" or "you"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Witylogix platform, APIs, dashboard, integrations, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must not use the Service.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Merchant Account Information

When you register for and use the Service, we collect:

- **Account details:** business name, contact name, email address, phone number, billing address
- **Authentication credentials:** hashed passwords, OAuth tokens from connected platforms
- **Billing information:** payment method details processed through Stripe (we do not store full credit card numbers)
- **Organization settings:** preferences, team member information, roles, and permissions

### 1.2 Integration Data

When you connect the Service to third-party platforms, we collect and process data from those integrations:

- **Shopify:** store name, order data, product information, customer names and delivery addresses, fulfillment status
- **WooCommerce:** store URL, order data, product details, customer shipping information, order status
- **Magento:** store configuration, order records, customer delivery information, catalog data

### 1.3 Order and Delivery Data

To provide logistics services, we process:

- **Order information:** order IDs, items, quantities, weights, dimensions, special handling instructions
- **Delivery addresses:** recipient names, street addresses, cities, postal codes, countries, phone numbers
- **Delivery status:** timestamps, proof of delivery, delivery notes, signatures

### 1.4 Driver and Fleet Data

- **Driver profiles:** names, contact information, vehicle details, license information
- **Location data:** real-time GPS coordinates of drivers during active deliveries
- **Performance metrics:** delivery times, completion rates, route efficiency data

### 1.5 Usage and Technical Data

- **Log data:** IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, timestamps
- **Device information:** device identifiers, screen resolution, language preferences
- **API usage:** request logs, endpoints called, response times, error rates
- **Performance data:** page load times, feature usage patterns, session duration

### 1.6 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and analyze usage. See Section 8 for details.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Service Providers (Sub-processors)

We engage trusted third parties to help operate the Service:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** | Cloud infrastructure and hosting | All service data (encrypted) |
| **Railway** | Application deployment and hosting | Application data (encrypted) |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Billing information, transaction data |

### 3.2 E-Commerce Platforms

When you authorize an integration, we share necessary data back to your connected platforms (e.g., fulfillment status updates to Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento).

### 3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Witylogix, our users, or the public.

### 3.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and any choices you may have regarding your information.

### 3.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Specific retention periods:

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Account information | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request |
| Order and delivery data | Duration of account + 90 days, or as required by law |
| Driver location data | 90 days after delivery completion |
| API logs | 12 months |
| Usage analytics | 24 months (aggregated) |
| Billing records | 7 years (legal requirement) |
| Backups | 30 days after data deletion from primary systems |

Upon account termination, we delete or anonymize your data in accordance with the timelines above, unless retention is required by law.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

### 5.1 Technical Measures

- **Encryption at rest:** All data stored in PostgreSQL and backups is encrypted using AES-256
- **Encryption in transit:** All communications use TLS 1.2 or higher
- **Access controls:** Role-based access control (RBAC) for all internal systems
- **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
- **Network security:** Firewalls, intrusion detection, and DDoS protection
- **Key management:** Encryption keys managed through secure key management services

### 5.2 Organizational Measures

- Security awareness training for all employees
- Background checks for personnel with access to production systems
- Incident response procedures and breach notification protocols
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Principle of least privilege for all system access

### 5.3 Infrastructure

- Production systems hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- Automated vulnerability scanning and patch management
- Comprehensive audit logging of all administrative actions
- Regular backup and disaster recovery testing

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 Rights Under GDPR (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request that we limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** File a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at [dpo@witylogix.io](mailto:dpo@witylogix.io). We will respond within 30 days.

For more details, see our [GDPR Compliance](/docs/legal/gdpr) page.

### 6.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with the following rights:

- **Right to Know:** Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale:** We do not sell personal information. If this changes, we will provide a "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at [legal@witylogix.io](mailto:legal@witylogix.io) or submit a request through your account dashboard. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

### 6.3 Rights Under Other Jurisdictions

We respect data protection rights under applicable laws worldwide, including but not limited to Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, and Australia's Privacy Act. Contact us to exercise your rights under any applicable data protection law.
