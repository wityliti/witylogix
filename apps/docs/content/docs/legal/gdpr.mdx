# GDPR Compliance

**Effective Date:** March 26, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 26, 2026

Witylogix Inc. ("Witylogix," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and rights of individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland. This page explains how Witylogix complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the UK GDPR.

---

## 1. Data Controller vs. Data Processor

Under the GDPR, different obligations apply depending on the role an organization plays in data processing:

### 1.1 Witylogix as Data Processor

For Merchant Data (orders, customer delivery addresses, driver information, and other data you submit through the Service), **your organization is the Data Controller** and **Witylogix acts as a Data Processor**. We process this data solely on your behalf and in accordance with your documented instructions.

Our obligations as a Data Processor are detailed in our [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/docs/legal/data-processing).

### 1.2 Witylogix as Data Controller

Witylogix acts as a Data Controller for:

- **Merchant account information:** registration data, billing information, and credentials for users of the Witylogix platform
- **Website visitor data:** data collected through our website via cookies and analytics
- **Business relationship data:** contact information for sales, support, and marketing communications

## 2. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following legal bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

### 2.1 When Witylogix is the Data Controller

| Legal Basis | Processing Activity |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Creating and managing your account, providing the Service, processing payments, delivering support |
| **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Improving the Service, ensuring security, preventing fraud, analytics for product development |
| **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) | Tax record-keeping, responding to lawful data access requests, complying with anti-money laundering regulations |
| **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) | Marketing communications, optional cookies and analytics (where consent is required) |

### 2.2 When Witylogix is the Data Processor

When processing Merchant Data on your behalf, the legal basis for processing is determined by you (the Data Controller). You are responsible for ensuring a valid legal basis exists for your processing activities. We process Merchant Data only in accordance with our DPA and your documented instructions.

## 3. Data Subject Rights

The GDPR grants individuals (data subjects) the following rights. How you exercise these rights depends on the context:

### 3.1 Rights Overview

| Right | Description | GDPR Article |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Right of Access** | Obtain a copy of your personal data and information about how it is processed | Art. 15 |
| **Right to Rectification** | Have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected | Art. 16 |
| **Right to Erasure** | Request deletion of personal data ("right to be forgotten") | Art. 17 |
| **Right to Restrict Processing** | Request that we limit how we use your data | Art. 18 |
| **Right to Data Portability** | Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format | Art. 20 |
| **Right to Object** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing | Art. 21 |
| **Right Not to Be Subject to Automated Decisions** | Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects | Art. 22 |

### 3.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

**If you are a Witylogix account holder (Witylogix is the Controller):**

Submit your request to our Data Protection Officer:

- **Email:** [dpo@witylogix.io](mailto:dpo@witylogix.io)
- **Subject line:** "GDPR Data Subject Request - [Right Name]"

We will:

1. Verify your identity within 3 business days
2. Acknowledge your request within 5 business days
3. Fulfill your request within 30 calendar days (extendable by 60 days for complex requests, with notification)

**If you are a customer of a Witylogix Merchant (Witylogix is the Processor):**

Your personal data (delivery address, name, phone number) is controlled by the Merchant you purchased from. You should direct your request to that Merchant. If a Merchant forwards a data subject request to us, we will assist them in fulfilling it in accordance with our DPA.

### 3.3 Verification

To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We may ask for:

- Confirmation of the email address associated with the account
- Additional identifying information if necessary

We will not fulfill requests that we cannot verify.

## 5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Witylogix is headquartered in the United States. When personal data is transferred from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to the United States or other countries that have not received an adequacy decision from the European Commission, we implement the following safeguards:

### 5.1 Transfer Mechanisms

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** We use the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor and Module 3: Processor-to-Processor) as approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914
- **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA):** For transfers from the UK, we use the UK IDTA or the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs as approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office
- **Swiss Transborder Data Flow Agreement:** For transfers from Switzerland, we rely on the SCCs as recognized by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner

### 5.2 Transfer Impact Assessments

We conduct transfer impact assessments to evaluate the legal framework of destination countries and implement supplementary measures where necessary, including:

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls limiting who can access personal data
- Contractual commitments from sub-processors
- Technical measures to prevent government access to data

## 7. Data Breach Notification

Witylogix maintains a comprehensive incident response plan for personal data breaches as required by Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

### 7.1 Notification to Data Controllers (Merchants)

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting Merchant Data, we will:

1. **Notify affected Merchants without undue delay** and no later than 48 hours after becoming aware of the breach
2. Provide the following information:
   - Nature of the breach, including categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
   - Name and contact details of the DPO
   - Likely consequences of the breach
   - Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects
3. Provide updated information as it becomes available
4. Cooperate with your investigation and assist you in fulfilling your obligations to supervisory authorities and data subjects

### 7.2 Notification to Supervisory Authorities

When Witylogix is the Data Controller, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals.

### 7.3 Notification to Data Subjects

When a breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we will communicate the breach to affected data subjects without undue delay, unless:

- We have implemented measures that render the data unintelligible (e.g., encryption)
- We have taken subsequent measures that ensure the high risk is no longer likely to materialize
- It would involve disproportionate effort, in which case a public communication is made

### 7.4 Incident Response

Our incident response process includes:

1. **Detection and containment:** Immediate technical measures to contain the breach
2. **Assessment:** Evaluation of the nature, scope, and severity of the breach
3. **Notification:** Timely notification to affected parties as described above
4. **Remediation:** Implementation of measures to prevent recurrence
5. **Documentation:** Comprehensive documentation of the breach, its effects, and remedial actions taken (maintained for a minimum of 5 years)

## 8. Data Protection by Design and Default

In accordance with Article 25 of the GDPR, Witylogix implements data protection by design and by default:

### 8.1 By Design

- Privacy considerations are integrated into the development lifecycle
- Data minimization: we collect only the data necessary for the specified purpose
- Pseudonymization and encryption are applied where appropriate
- Regular privacy impact assessments for new features and processing activities

### 8.2 By Default

- Only data necessary for each specific processing purpose is collected
- Data is not made accessible to an indefinite number of persons without authorization
- Default retention periods are applied, with data deleted when no longer needed
- Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis

As required by Article 30 of the GDPR, Witylogix maintains records of processing activities for both our roles as Controller and Processor. These records are available to supervisory authorities upon request.
